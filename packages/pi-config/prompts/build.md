## /build — Design-then-deliver workflow

Idea: $@

**Phase 1 — Brainstorm**
1. Read existing code/docs to understand the domain.
2. State your understanding of what I want. Ask clarifying questions if the idea is ambiguous.
3. Propose 2-3 approaches. For each: tradeoffs, effort estimate, and the simplest viable path.

**Phase 2 — Plan**
1. Sequence the work into concrete steps. Use a task list.
2. Call out dependencies, risks, and what could go wrong.
3. Stop and wait for my go-ahead before coding.

**Phase 3 — Execute**
1. Implement step by step, smallest-first.
2. Verify each step with tests or manual checks before moving on.
3. Summarise what was built, what changed, and what's left.

Restart if I say "pivot". The plan is a draft, not a prison.
